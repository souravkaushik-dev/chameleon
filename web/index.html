What Chameleon already does.

The beta is real and usable — here's what's already shipped.

Cinematic player

Full-screen, artwork-focused playback with smooth seek controls and a mini player that's always one tap from expanding.

Liquid glass navigation

A floating nav bar with real glass magnification, refraction, and a jelly-morph animation on every selection.

A library that's yours

Liked songs, custom playlists, and recently played — all persisted locally, no account required.

Real search

Fast lookup with recent search history, so you're rarely starting from a blank box.

Light & dark themes

Animated, system-aware theme switching built in from the start — not bolted on later.

Queue & playlist actions

Add to queue or to any playlist straight from a song's action menu — like and organize without leaving your flow.
