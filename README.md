## ✦ About

Chameleon is a minimal music player built around a simple idea:

> **Music, without the noise.**

The iOS version brings the Chameleon experience to Apple devices using **Flutter**, while continuing the design and product direction established by the original **Android version**.

The Android version remains the original platform and the primary reference for the Chameleon experience.

The iOS app is currently **behind the Android version in feature parity and development**.

---

## ◉ Features

* ▶︎ Music playback
* ⌕ Music search
* ♫ Playlists
* ☷ Queue management
* ⏮ Previous / Next controls
* ◉ Background audio
* 🔒 Lock Screen controls
* 🎛 Control Center media controls
* ◇ Album artwork
* ━ Playback progress
* ⚡ Stream caching
* ⏭ Next-song preloading

## ✧ The Experience

Chameleon keeps the interface quiet and the music prominent.

**Artwork first.
Controls when needed.
Nothing extra.**

The iOS version follows the visual language and core experience of the original Android application while adapting the interface and behavior for iOS.

## 🐛 Known Issues

### Song Playback

Some songs may occasionally fail to start when selected from certain sections, playlists, or queues.

### Song Duration

Some songs may display an incorrect or duplicated duration.

Example:

```text
2:03
4:06
```

These issues are known and may be addressed in future releases.
